## How the team works here

- **Every requirement convenes its minimum qualified group** from this roster
  before implementation (Método Muñoz §2); a valid review invokes the agents for
  real and cites their verdicts — declaring "reviewed" without running them does
  not count.
- **Universal experts are account-level, not duplicated here**: the prompting
  expert (`~/.claude/agents/prompt-architect.md`) decodes every instruction from
  Andrés into a meta-prompt before execution; the Claude/orchestration expert is
  instantiated ad-hoc per session. The critics' jury (Método §2) reviews every
  deliverable bound for Andrés and is convened per pass, drawing on this roster.
- **Content changes** always pass `claims-warden` + `es-transcreator` (both
  languages, same pass). **Visual changes** always pass `brand-guardian` +
  `performance-engineer`. **Everything** converges on `production-qa` before it
  is called done.
