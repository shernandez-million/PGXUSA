What we do

Six ways we improve a home.

We don't chase every kind of job. We remodel, extend, and upgrade existing homes — and we handle the paperwork that makes it legal.

01 Whole-home remodeling

Full-gut renovations and major layout changes. We open walls, relocate plumbing and electrical, modernize systems, and deliver a home that works the way you live now — not the way it was drawn in 1975.

Layout changesStructural openingsFull MEP updatesFlooring & finishesImpact windows & doors
02 Additions & extensions

More house without moving. Rear extensions, second stories, garage conversions, and in-law suites — engineered, tied into the existing structure correctly, and permitted from day one.

Second storiesRear extensionsGarage conversionsTie-beam upgradesFoundation work
03 Kitchens

The room that decides how a home feels — and what it's worth. Custom cabinetry, stone counters, appliance and fixture relocation, and lighting that makes the whole space work.

Custom cabinetryStone countersIslands & layoutAppliance relocationLighting design
04 Bathrooms

Primary baths, guest baths, and powder rooms. Frameless glass, freestanding tubs, stone and tile — with the waterproofing details that nobody sees but everybody regrets skipping.

Primary suitesFrameless showersFreestanding tubsStone & tileWaterproofing

South Florida living happens outside. Terraces, summer kitchens, pergolas, and driveways — plus the exterior envelope work that protects everything: stucco, paint, roofing coordination, and impact openings.
