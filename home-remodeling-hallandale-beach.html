Home Remodeling in Hallandale Beach

Two housing markets, one zip code.

Hallandale Beach runs two parallel housing markets. East of the Intracoastal, South Ocean Drive is a wall of towers, from the 1960s and 70s buildings around the Hemispheres and Parker Plaza to the newer generation at the Beach Club and 2000 Ocean. West of the bridge sit the low-rise neighborhoods: mid-century houses on the canals of Golden Isles, the condo and townhouse communities off Diana Drive in Three Islands, and the older streets near Atlantic Shores Boulevard. A whole-home remodel here means one of two very different jobs.

Both run through the City of Hallandale Beach Building Division, one of the divisions inside the Department of Sustainable Development, which enforces the Florida Building Code together with Broward's administrative amendments. That second layer is the Broward difference: the county Board of Rules and Appeals interprets the code, hears appeals of building official decisions, and reviews local amendments, so the answer here is not automatically the Miami-Dade answer. Broward sits inside the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so impact windows and doors still need HVHZ product approvals. In a tower, the association's approval comes before any of it.

More than twenty years of South Florida construction sit behind PGX Builders Group, along with 150-plus delivered remodels and additions, each one permitted and inspected. In Hallandale Beach that means a fixed written scope before the first wall comes down, a supervisor on site every day, and crews who book the service elevator instead of arguing with the front desk about it. Elevator pads, corridor protection and a clean hallway at five o'clock are part of the job, not a favor. One team carries it from estimate through warranty.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Who has to approve a full remodel in a Hallandale Beach condo?
Two parties, in order. Your association reviews the plans, the contractor's license and insurance, the work hours and the elevator schedule, and many boards here restrict heavy construction to the off-season. Then the City of Hallandale Beach Building Division reviews for code and issues permits, with inspections along the way. We submit to the board first, because a permit set the association later rejects is a set you paid to draw twice.
What is the Broward County Board of Rules and Appeals and why does it matter to me?
It is the county body behind building code administration in Broward: it considers local code amendments, issues formal interpretations when a code section is disputed, and hears appeals of a building official's decision. For you it mostly stays invisible, but it is the reason a detail waved through in Miami-Dade can still draw a comment in Hallandale Beach. Contractors who work both counties without noticing the difference collect rejection letters.
My building is going through its milestone inspection. Should I wait to remodel?
Usually not, but talk to the board first. Milestone inspections and structural integrity reserve studies examine the building's structure and common elements, not your kitchen, and they rarely stop unit work. What they can do is trigger concrete restoration, balcony repairs or a special assessment that competes for the same elevators and the same calendar. We ask the property manager what is scheduled before building a construction sequence around it.
What is a realistic timeline for gutting a whole unit or house?
Design and permitting usually take a few months before anyone swings a hammer, and association review adds its own weeks. Construction on a full-floor condo commonly runs several months; a canal house in Golden Isles with structural work and new openings runs longer. Seasonal restrictions on construction hours can stretch the calendar further. You get a written schedule with the estimate and a supervisor whose job is defending it.
What should a high-end remodel in Hallandale Beach cost?
It depends far more on what is behind the walls than on square footage. A tower unit with original plumbing stacks, no impact glass and a slab that limits where drains can go is a different budget from a recently built residence needing finishes. We price a written specification after walking the space, so the number you sign is the number you pay. We are rarely the cheapest of three bids, and we say so early.
